Core Mathematical Formulation

1. Angle Embedding Feature Map

Maps classical normalized features \(x_i \in [0, \pi]\) to single-qubit rotations:

R_y(x_i) = \begin{pmatrix} \cos(x_i/2) & -\sin(x_i/2) \\ \sin(x_i/2) & \cos(x_i/2) \end{pmatrix}

Cyclical time features \(\sin/\cos(\text{hour})\) map natively onto Bloch sphere rotations.

2. Strongly Entangling Ansatz & CNOT Gates

Applies parameterized rotations \(R_x(\theta_1) R_y(\theta_2) R_z(\theta_3)\) and CNOT entanglement:

U(\theta) = \prod_{l=1}^L \left( \text{CNOT}_{i,i+1} \cdot \bigotimes_{k=1}^8 R(\theta_{l,k}) \right)
3. Quantum Kernel Overlap Metric

Measures inner product similarity between customer state vectors in Hilbert space:

K(x_i, x_j) = |\langle \psi(x_i) \mid \psi(x_j) \rangle|^2
💡 Winning Framing: Quantum Inductive Bias
We do not claim 'Quantum Supremacy' on 8 qubits (256 amplitudes is classically trivial). We demonstrate that Quantum Kernel state overlaps provide a beneficial inductive bias for non-linear retail data correlations at equal parameter budgets (~72 parameters).

❓ Judges Q&A Defense Cheat Sheet

Master answers to the top 5 questions judges will ask during the pitch.

Q1: Why 8 qubits? Is 8 qubits computationally quantum superior?
Answer: No. 8 qubits represent 2^8 = 256 state vector amplitudes, which is classically trivial to simulate. We do not claim quantum supremacy. 8 qubits represents our top 8 quantum-aware retail features, allowing fast, exact GPU backprop training while proving quantum inductive bias.
Q2: Did you achieve quantum advantage over classical models?
Answer: We demonstrate quantum inductive bias--showing that quantum feature maps learn complex non-linear retail correlations with equal parameter budgets (~72 parameters) and maintain higher accuracy on small dataset sizes.
Q3: Why PennyLane over Qiskit?
Answer: PennyLane integrates natively with PyTorch via qml.qnn.TorchLayer, enabling end-to-end backpropagation differentiation across both quantum circuits and classical post-processing layers.
Q4: How do you know your quantum kernel isn't just an RBF kernel?
Answer: We calculated the Frobenius kernel alignment score between our quantum kernel matrix and a classical RBF kernel. Our alignment score of ~0.632 proves the quantum kernel is learning a distinct representation.
Q5: What happens if the quantum simulator stalls in production?
Answer: We built a Graceful Fallback Circuit in FastAPI. If quantum execution exceeds 200ms, the API automatically switches to the pre-trained PyTorch MLP model with zero downtime.
